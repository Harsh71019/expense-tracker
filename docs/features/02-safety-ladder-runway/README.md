# Safety Ladder and Financial Runway

## Outcome

Convert verified essential spending and explicitly eligible liquid reserves into survival time, then evaluate a sequential safety ladder with transparent unmet conditions and a recovery path after reserve use.

## Safety stages

- **Ground zero:** protection facts are complete and no declared high-cost debt remains unresolved.
- **Building fortress:** emergency runway is below the configured target.
- **Buffer layer:** emergency target is met and short-term sinking funds are being built.
- **Wealth ready:** safety requirements are satisfied; this enables planning candidates, not automatic aggressive investing.

The interface shows a stage, checklist, data confidence, and evidence. It does not show a false-precision decimal score.

## Subfeatures

1. Essential-burn calculation
2. Emergency-reserve source classification
3. Safety evaluation and runway clock
4. Break-glass recovery plan

## Existing capabilities to reuse

Posted transactions, essential/lifestyle category grouping, assets and valuations, account balances, reports, goals, scheduled jobs, notification outbox, and dashboard components.

## Key dependency

Runway is unavailable until there is a positive essential burn and at least one eligible reserve source. Limited history produces an explicitly limited estimate rather than an apparently exact result.
